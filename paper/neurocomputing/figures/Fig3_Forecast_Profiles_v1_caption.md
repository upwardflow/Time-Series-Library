# Figure 3 caption

**Fig. 3 | Real forecast profiles at short and long horizons.** **a,b** ETTm1 OT forecasts from TimeRole and RGSP-96 at horizons 96 and 720. **c,d** ETTh1 OT forecasts from TimeRole and DLinear at the same horizons. Each row reuses one deterministic test origin selected from ground-truth variability only (nearest to the median total variation among valid 720-step origins), and the 96-step target is the prefix of the corresponding 720-step target. Gray shading marks the last 96 observed points and the dashed line marks forecast onset. Window MAE is reported in the original data scale as TimeRole/comparison model. These individual windows provide qualitative inspection and do not replace full-test aggregate results.
