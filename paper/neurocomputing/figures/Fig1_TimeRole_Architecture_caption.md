**Fig. 1 | Role-differentiated TimeRole architecture.** **a** The recent window is processed by RGSP to produce the base forecast, while compressed distant history is processed by DHC to produce a conditional correction; both outputs are added in forecast space. **b** RGSP combines decomposition, dual-scale patches, a shared bidirectional Mamba encoder and variable-graph propagation. **c** DHC uses recent-reference normalization, distant-history pooling, paired shared decoding and a bounded variable gate.
