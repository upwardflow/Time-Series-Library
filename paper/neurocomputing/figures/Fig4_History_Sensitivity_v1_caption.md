# Figure 4 caption

**Fig. 4 | Sensitivity to history range and resolution allocation.** Test-MSE change relative to the standard setting (total history 336, recent window 96, pooling width 16) when varying **a** total history length, **b** the recent high-resolution window, and **c** distant-history pooling width. Positive values indicate worse performance. Small translucent markers are dataset-level means over three seeds; large connected markers are horizon-wise macro means. ETTm1, ETTm2 and Weather are included in **a**; ETTm1 and ETTm2 are included in **b,c**. Longer total histories preferentially improve H=720, enlarging the recent path is not consistently beneficial, and pooling-width effects are small over the tested range. Markers show observed task means, not confidence intervals.
