# Figure 2 caption (v2)

**Fig. 2 | Evidence for distant-history conditional correction.** **a** Paired test-set improvements of TimeRole over RGSP-96 on ETTm1 and ETTm2 at four prediction horizons. Each small marker is one paired seed, the larger marker is the task mean, and the horizontal segment spans the three observed seed values. Blue circles and orange squares denote MSE and MAE, respectively. Improvements are calculated as \((e_{\mathrm{baseline}}-e_{\mathrm{DHC}})/e_{\mathrm{baseline}}\times100\%\), so positive values favour TimeRole; all 24 paired runs improve both metrics. **b** Relative validation-MSE change after replacing intact distant history with sample mismatch, temporal shuffle, temporal reversal, recent-mean replacement or noise replacement. Values are obtained from one frozen checkpoint (seed 2021); positive values indicate degradation relative to intact history. The outlined mismatch column is the largest degradation for all four evaluated tasks. **c** Paired test-set improvements of TimeXer+DHC over TimeXer-336 under the same convention as in **a**. All 12 paired runs are displayed, including negative transfer (MSE wins, 10/12; MAE wins, 8/12). Horizontal segments show observed seed ranges, not confidence intervals; no inferential test is reported. Source data are provided with the figure.
