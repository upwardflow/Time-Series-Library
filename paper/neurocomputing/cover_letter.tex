Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript entitled ``TimeRole: Role-Differentiated
Historical Modeling for Multivariate Long-Term Time-Series Forecasting'' for
publication as a Research Article in \textit{Neurocomputing}.

The manuscript introduces TimeRole, which separates high-resolution recent-state
forecasting from compressed distant-history correction. This design gives recent
and distant observations distinct predictive responsibilities and allows the model
to exploit a longer accessible history without expanding the expensive recent-state
predictor over the full input.

Across five datasets and four prediction horizons under a unified three-seed
evaluation, TimeRole obtains 12 best MSE and 11 best MAE results. The manuscript
also evaluates seed-level stability, historical interventions, transfer across
forecasting backbones, and the accuracy--efficiency trade-off.
